SAH V30.6
=========
- Added a hard participation gate in Student Portal.
- If the student has 2 or more open beneficiary-satisfaction surveys that have not been submitted, all Apply Now / participation buttons are blocked.
- Clicking a blocked participation button does not submit an application.
- The oldest pending survey opens automatically to help the student clear the restriction.
- A branded warning panel appears in Student Portal showing the number of pending surveys.
- As soon as enough surveys are completed and pending count drops below 2, participation buttons are automatically re-enabled.
- Survey gate persists because eligibility and completed surveys are stored in localStorage.
- JavaScript syntax validation passed.
